# freqlist
CSV files with analog voice radio communication frequency lists for use in CHIRP and other such software, primarily for Finland and other Nordic countries in Europe.

# Important Legal Notice
Both receiving (listening) as well as transceiving (broadcasting/talking) on any of the listed frequencies may be subject to legal regulations not mentioned here. This site only provides general, high-level Information. It is not complete nor accurate in many respects. **Always make sure from an official source, independent from this site, that both receiving and transceiving on any of the listed frequencies is legal in your specific case.**

# CSV Files

## PMR446 EU

Private Mobile Radio on 446 MHz. Can be used without license in most European countries for personal and business use but only with radios specifically for PMR446.

Use of 67.0 Hz or other CTCSS subtone is common in pre-programmed radios.

Channel 8 is used as Calling Channel.

Source: Wikipedia, Finlex
